Antonio Rodriguez

Our Purpose: To honor God by helping leaders and organizations, small and vast, anchor their work in truth, beauty, goodness, and depth. The more the work means to you, the more it means to the world.

“Most leaders can tell you what the company earned last quarter. Fewer can tell you why the work matters, or what their people are becoming. I work with leaders until those things come back into view: the power and importance of one’s work, the deep meaning in it and in the people it serves.”

Antonio Rodriguez has spent more than 25 years designing and leading the rooms where leaders and teams learn, decide, and change. He has been blessed to work with many extraordinary schools, foundations, nonprofits, and businesses across the Americas. He specializes in custom program design, train-the-trainer systems, and facilitation at scale; at the Latin American Leadership Academy, he trained more than 100 facilitators and 2,000 young leaders across six countries. He lives and works in Puerto Rico. He believes every person is of infinite worth, and that work held in that belief can do for people what a great mentor does.
